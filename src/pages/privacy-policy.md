# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 1, 2025**

## Tangle Demo (Shopify/HuggingFace)

Our demo application is provided by Shopify and hosted on HuggingFace. When you use the Tangle demo, your data is subject to Shopify's Privacy Policy:

**[Shopify Privacy Policy](https://www.shopify.com/ca/legal/privacy)**

## Open Source / Self-Hosted Installations

If you're running Tangle locally or self-hosting the open-source code:

- **We do not collect or store any user data**
- The only data collection is through Google Analytics on this website for basic usage statistics
- All your data remains on your local machine or infrastructure

## Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage information such as pages visited and time spent on site.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
